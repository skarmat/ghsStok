Academic Excellence

Empowering Learners Through a Balanced & Future-Ready Curriculum

At Govt. High School Stok, our academic framework is affiliated with the Jammu & Kashmir Board of School Education (JKBOSE) and strictly follows the NCERT (National Council of Educational Research and Training) guidelines.

We blend national curriculum standards with contextual Himalayan knowledge—integrating environmental conservation, digital literacy, and local culture. Our goal is to cultivate analytical thinking, creativity, and strong moral character in every student.

Assessment System

Evaluation & Grading Scheme

Following the Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE) framework prescribed by JKBOSE.

T1

Formative Assessments (FA)

Conducted continuously throughout the term through class tests, unit assignments, oral quizzes, projects, and lab activities.

  • Regular Unit Tests
  • Subject Enrichment Activities
  • Homework & Attendance Marks
Key Milestone
SA

Summative Assessments (SA)

Term-End comprehensive written examinations conducted at mid-session and end-session to evaluate cumulative understanding.

  • Term-I (Autumn Examination)
  • Term-II (Annual Examination)
  • Class X Board Exams (JKBOSE Conducted)
Co

Co-Scholastic Evaluation

Assessment of life skills, discipline, moral values, environmental sensitivity, participation in games, and cultural activities.

  • 5-Point Grading Scale (A to E)
  • Eco-Club & Social Work
  • Sports & Physical Fitness
Session Roadmap

Academic Calendar 2026–27

Note: Academic session in Ladakh follows the traditional March–November operational window with winter vacation during peak winter.

March 2026

Session Commencement

School reopens after winter break. Distribution of textbooks, orientation, and regular classes begin.

July 2026

Mid-Term Examinations

Completion of 50% syllabus, Unit Test 2, and Mid-Term PTM with parents.

October 2026

Pre-Board & Annual Exams

Class X Pre-Board exams and Annual Examinations for Classes VI to IX prior to severe winter setting in.

Dec – Feb

Winter Vacation / Camp

Official winter break. Winter coaching camps organized for Class X Board candidates.
