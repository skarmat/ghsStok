Our Heritage

A Legacy of Excellence
Since 1939

Established in the majestic valley of Stok, Government High School Stok has been a cornerstone of educational development in the region for nearly five decades. What began as a humble primary school with just two classrooms has blossomed into a premier institution serving the youth of Ladakh.

We are deeply rooted in the cultural ethos of our land, yet we continuously evolve to meet the demands of modern education. Affiliated with the Jammu and Kashmir Board of School Education (JKBOSE), we offer a rigorous academic curriculum blended seamlessly with local culture, sports, and life skills.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be a beacon of transformative education that empowers students to become enlightened citizens, capable of fostering global harmony while remaining deeply connected to their cultural roots.

Our Mission

  • To provide a holistic, student-centric, and inclusive learning environment.
  • To inculcate values of integrity, empathy, and resilience in every child.
  • To bridge the educational gap by bringing modern pedagogies to rural Ladakh.
Message from the Desk

From the Headmaster

Welcome to Government High School Stok. It gives me immense pleasure to greet you on behalf of our dedicated staff, enthusiastic students, and supportive community.

Education in the 21st century goes beyond textbooks and examinations. In a rapidly changing world, it is our responsibility to equip our students not just with academic knowledge, but with critical thinking, emotional intelligence, and moral fortitude. Here at GHS Stok, we strive to create an ecosystem where every child feels valued and inspired.

Being situated in the remote yet breathtaking region of Ladakh presents unique challenges and profound opportunities. We take pride in our robust curriculum that adheres to national standards while ensuring that our students remain deeply appreciative of their rich local heritage and environmental responsibilities.

My vision for this institution is to see it become a center of excellence where rural youth are not at a disadvantage, but rather, are empowered by their unique perspectives. We encourage parents to be active partners in this journey. Together, we can shape the leaders, innovators, and compassionate human beings of tomorrow.

Mr. Padma Tsering

Headmaster, Govt. High School Stok
