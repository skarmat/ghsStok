Welcome to GHS Stok

Nurturing Potential, Building Character Since 1939.

Nestled in the serene landscape of Stok, our school has been a beacon of learning for decades. We believe in providing holistic education that blends modern pedagogical approaches with the rich cultural heritage of Ladakh.

Our dedicated faculty ensures that every child is given the attention they need to thrive academically, socially, and emotionally. With state-of-the-art facilities, a robust curriculum, and a focus on extracurricular activities, we prepare our students to face the challenges of tomorrow with confidence and integrity.
