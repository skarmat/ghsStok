How to Get Admitted

We strive to keep our admission process transparent, straightforward, and supportive for parents and students.

01

Submit Application

Fill out the digital form below or visit our school admin office to pick up a physical copy.

02

Doc Verification

Bring original documents (Aadhaar, Birth Certificate, Previous Marksheet) to the school office.

03

Interaction & Review

A brief, friendly interaction with the student and parents to understand learning needs.

04

Confirmation

Zero Financial Barrier

Fees & Government Welfare Schemes

As a Government institution under UT Ladakh Administration, we provide free education and comprehensive welfare support to all students.

Zero Tuition Fee

Education is 100% free for all students from Class VI to Class X. No monthly tuition fee or admission capitation charges are levied.

Guaranteed by Education Dept.

Free Books & Uniforms

Under Samagra Shiksha Scheme, every student receives free NCERT textbooks, notebooks, school uniforms, and winter kit support.
